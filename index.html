Flip San

A gravity-flip merge game for iPhone and iPad.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026
Developer: Sam B
Contact: sambhate@protonmail.com

Flip San is a single-player merge game. It does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information — from adults or from children.

Specifically:

  • No accounts. Flip San has no sign-in, no user profile, and no password.
  • No data collection. The game does not collect names, email addresses, locations, device identifiers, contacts, photos, or gameplay analytics.
  • No third-party SDKs. There are no advertising, analytics, or tracking libraries in the game.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases. Nothing in the game sends data to an ad network or payment provider.
  • No network use. The game works fully offline. It makes no network calls and sends nothing to any server.
  • On-device only. Scores and game state live only on the player's own device and are never transmitted anywhere.

Because Flip San collects no data, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete. If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here and the change will be noted in the App Store listing.
